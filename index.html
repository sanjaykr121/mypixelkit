Compress Image

FastSize controlPrivacy safe
  • How it works: Re-encodes images in your browser using canvas and WebP/JPEG compression, with adjustable quality and a target file size.

Custom dimensionsAspect ratioPrivacy safe
  • How it works: Changes pixel width and height, with a lock-aspect-ratio option and quick presets.

Format Converter

JPGPNGWebP / AVIF*
  • How it works: Exports images from a browser canvas to JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF when supported by your browser.

Passport Photo Maker

413 × 531 px300 DPI sizePrivacy safe
  • How it works: Center-crops to a 3.5 × 4.5 cm proportion and exports at 413 × 531 px, with white or light-blue fill for transparent areas.

  • How it works: Trims blank edges, boosts grayscale contrast, and compresses toward a 20 KB target.

Privacy Policy

Your images stay on your device. MyPixelKit processes selected images locally in your browser using browser APIs such as Canvas. Image files are not uploaded to MyPixelKit servers, and the app does not store them remotely.

Local files and downloads

Files you choose are used only in the current browser session to create the result you request. You control whether to save or share downloaded results. Closing the page clears the app's in-memory working data.

Website requests

Loading this website still makes ordinary requests to the web host for its pages and app files. The host may process standard technical request logs under its own policies. This is separate from image processing; your selected image contents are not sent with those requests.

Your control

You can stop using the tools at any time and clear this site's browser data using your browser settings. No online account is needed to process images.

MyPixelKit provides browser-based image utilities for personal and professional workflows. Your selected images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to MyPixelKit for processing.

Use and results

You are responsible for having the rights to use the files you select and for reviewing each output before using or sharing it. Processing quality and supported formats can vary by browser and device.

Important checks

Photo, DPI, size, and document requirements differ by agency and submission portal. Verify the latest official requirements before submitting an image. Tools are provided as-is and are not a guarantee that an image will be accepted.

Availability

Features may depend on browser support, available device memory, and file size. Keep your originals and backups; downloaded results are under your control.
